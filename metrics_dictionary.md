# Marketing Unit Economics — Metrics Dictionary

This document describes the key metrics used in the Marketing Unit Economics project.

| Metric | Full Name | Formula | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visitors | Website Visitors | — | Number of users who visited the website through an advertising channel. |
| CPC | Cost Per Click | Ad Spend / Clicks | Average cost of one advertising click. In this educational model, it represents cost per visitor. |
| Ad Spend | Advertising Spend | Visitors × Cost per Visitor | Total amount spent on advertising. |
| Orders | Number of Orders | — | Total number of orders generated by an advertising channel. |
| CR | Conversion Rate | Orders / Visitors × 100% | Percentage of visitors who completed an order. |
| CAC | Customer Acquisition Cost | Ad Spend / Orders | Average advertising cost required to generate one order. |
| AOV | Average Order Value | Revenue / Orders | Average revenue generated by one order. |
| Revenue | Total Revenue | Orders × AOV | Total sales revenue generated by a channel. |
| COGS | Cost of Goods Sold | Orders × COGS per Order | Direct cost of the goods sold. |
| Gross Profit | Gross Profit | Revenue − COGS | Revenue remaining after deducting the cost of goods sold. |
| Profit After Marketing | Profit After Marketing | Gross Profit − Ad Spend | Gross profit remaining after advertising expenses. |
| ROMI | Return on Marketing Investment | (Gross Profit − Ad Spend) / Ad Spend × 100% | Measures the return on advertising investment relative to advertising spend. |

## Important Notes

- The dataset is simulated and used for educational purposes.
- CPC is used as cost per visitor in this model because click-level data is not available.
- Orders are used as a simplified approximation of acquired customers when calculating CAC.
- Profit After Marketing is not net profit because operating expenses, salaries, taxes, and other costs are excluded.
- ROMI is calculated using gross profit rather than total revenue.

## Project Scope

The purpose of these metrics is to compare advertising channels, evaluate acquisition efficiency, measure profitability, and support data-driven budget allocation decisions.
